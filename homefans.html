The challenge

Homefans connects football fans with local hosts who take them to the match: matchdays in Rio and Buenos Aires, derbies in Istanbul, weekends in Milan. Fans book from more than 60 countries, so demand is global and supply is local.

That means marketing can't only push demand. New destinations need hosts before they can be sold, sales and marketing need one plan, and a small team has to run paid, social and email across many markets at once without things falling through the cracks.

Product · landing pages

Club pages: find your match, not a list of look-alikes

Most Homefans traffic comes from Google Ads on ticket searches. Fans land looking for one game, but find many experiences with almost identical names. So I designed a club landing page that answers the search: this club's home matches, and what you can book for each.

01

Grouped by home fixture

Experiences sit under the match they include, so fans pick the game first and the experience second.

02

Low dev effort, built in bulk

The page reuses data the backend already has and only changes the interface, so it can be generated for the most popular clubs at once.

03

Trust signals in green

Research into the big travel platforms showed our site lacked trust signals. A green trust bar answers the main worries: real tickets, instant confirmation, a local host.
