The challenge

Katalysta is an AI-native education platform for high-stakes career switching. It pairs a 24/7 AI Tutor for instant code reviews with 1:1 strategy calls with industry mentors: a flexible but rigorous path into tech.

When we launched in Georgia, we had a high-volume, low-intent problem. Ads brought plenty of visitors, but conversion was only 0.7%. We started with a single funnel and generic messaging about the benefits of an IT career, which didn't address the barriers actually stopping people from making the switch.

What I did

From more traffic to better conversion

New lead magnets and email flows that tackled the specific fears people had about changing careers.

Generic “why IT” messaging

Optimizing for conversion
01

A simpler entry point

60→30 minwebinar

Cut the on-demand webinar in half. Instead of a long lecture, it showed a quick “Career Blueprint” and a live demo of the AI Tutor solving a real problem, proving the product's value immediately.

02

Concrete messaging

Dropped generic promises and talked real numbers: specific salary trajectories for developers in the local market. That reframed the course from an expense into a clear career investment.

03

Behavior-based follow-ups

Automated emails triggered by how each person interacted with the content, addressing their specific hesitations in real time.

My role

I owned end-to-end execution of the marketing funnel: core messaging, technical setup, ad campaigns, and iterating on real-time data. I also worked closely with the team to develop and adapt the go-to-market strategy for the Georgian market.
