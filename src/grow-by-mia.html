The challenge

Grow by Mia is a Swedish digital education brand supporting women through online programs. When I joined as part of the founding team, it had a strong social following but almost no marketing infrastructure: 800 email contacts, no automation and little ad history.

The goal was to grow the list, generate steady revenue from paid and free webinars, and enroll 100+ women into the €5,000 flagship program.
