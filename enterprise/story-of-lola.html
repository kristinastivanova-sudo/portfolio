Doubling Black Friday sales with a staged paid-social campaign

A three-stage campaign (build the audience, warm it up, convert) for a fashion e-commerce brand, plus a homepage fix that lifted store conversion from 0.1% to 1.13%.

Role
Paid social & campaign strategy
Timeline

The challenge

Story of Lola is a small UK fashion brand. With two weeks to go before Black Friday, it had a Shopify customer list, no ad history and very little budget.

The plan had to reach new people fast, warm them up, and have an audience ready to buy when the offer went live.

What I did

Three stages in three weeks

01

Build the audience

Two cold campaigns, UK and Germany, based on the Shopify customer list. 25,600+ people reached for £120; every add to cart came from Instagram, so Facebook was paused.

02

Warm it up

People saved the ads but waited for a deal, so I retargeted them with a limited 20% offer and collected emails for the main sale, for £17.56.

03

Sell

Ran the Black Friday ads on Instagram only, with a homepage banner and an email to customers and the new leads.

04

Fix the leak

When the sale went live, conversion fell to 0.1%: the homepage didn’t show the offer. I briefed a new banner with the code, and conversion rose to 1.13%.
